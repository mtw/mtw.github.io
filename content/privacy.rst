Hosting and server logs
=======================

The site is a set of static files served by **GitHub Pages** (GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P.
Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA). When you open a page, your browser
sends the technical data needed to deliver it: your IP address, the requested address,
the time, the referring page, and browser and operating system identifiers. GitHub
processes these data in server logs to operate and secure the service; see the
`GitHub Privacy Statement <https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement>`_
and the information on `GitHub Pages data collection <https://docs.github.com/en/pages/getting-started-with-github-pages/about-github-pages#data-collection>`_.
Legal basis: legitimate interest in operating a secure website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
GitHub, Inc. is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

Fonts are served from this site's own domain; opening a page does not contact a font
provider or any other third party.

Cookies and consent
===================

The site sets **no cookies and stores nothing in your browser** unless you accept
analytics in the banner shown on your first visit. Your choice, whether you accept or
decline, is remembered in your browser's local storage (key ``mtw-consent``) for one
year so that the banner does not reappear. Declining has no effect on the site.

You can change or withdraw your choice at any time via the **Cookie settings** link in
the footer of every page. If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, the
banner is not shown and analytics stay off.

Web analytics (only with your consent)
======================================

If you accept, the site loads **Google Analytics 4**, a service of Google Ireland
Limited (Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland). Nothing from Google is
requested before you accept. Google Analytics then sets cookies (``_ga``, ``_ga_*``,
valid for up to two years) and collects pseudonymous usage data: pages viewed, time and
duration of the visit, the referring site, approximate location derived from the IP
address (Google Analytics 4 does not log or store the full IP address), and
device, browser, and screen information. I use these reports only to understand which
content is read.

Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and § 165(3) of the Austrian
Telecommunications Act, TKG 2021). Google may transfer the data to Google LLC in the
United States; Google LLC is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, and the
transfer is additionally covered by the EU standard contractual clauses in Google's
data-processing terms. Event-level data are retained by Google Analytics for 14 months
and then only as aggregated reports. Further information: `How Google uses information
from sites that use its services <https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites>`_
and the `Google Privacy Policy <https://policies.google.com/privacy>`_. You can also
block Google Analytics in every browser with the `Google Analytics opt-out add-on
<https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout>`_.

Embedded third-party content
============================

One page embeds a video from YouTube in privacy-enhanced mode
(``youtube-nocookie.com``, Google Ireland Limited). Opening that page loads the player
from Google, which then sees your IP address and browser data; cookies are set only
when you start playback. Legal basis: legitimate interest in presenting the recorded
conversation (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Google's privacy policy applies to that processing.
No other page loads content from third parties.

Links to other sites
====================

The site links to publishers, DOI resolvers, repositories, and profile pages. Those
sites are operated by third parties and process your data under their own policies.

Your rights
===========

Under the GDPR you have the right to access the personal data concerning you, to have
them rectified or erased, to restrict or object to their processing, to data
portability, and to withdraw a consent at any time with effect for the future. To
exercise these rights, write to the e-mail address above. You also have the right to
lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority; in Austria this is the
`Datenschutzbehörde <https://www.dsb.gv.at>`_, Barichgasse 40-42, 1030 Vienna.
